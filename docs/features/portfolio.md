## Reach

Portfolio in the header opens a small pane of the cards' frosted glass under it, offering Web Apps and Miscellaneous; it slides down 3rem into place, starting to fade in as it moves, and closes on a choice, a click outside or Escape, and Portfolio stays underlined on both pages (`components/Header/NavDropdown.tsx`). In the phone menu Portfolio is a button: it turns the menu to a second view listing the two, with a way back (`components/Header/MobileNav/index.tsx`; the menu slides down from above as it opens, fading in on the way, `animate-menu-in` in `tailwind.config.js`), and stays underlined on both pages; the menu's glass is tinted deeper than a card's (`data-glass-deep`, `components/Background/glass.ts`) so its square lenses show plainly, and it covers the footer's glass. `/portfolio` itself redirects to Web Apps. Web Apps and Miscellaneous list their posts from `Contents.tsx` beside them; on a wide screen, sliding chevrons (`components/PageScroller/Chevrons.tsx`, shrunk) pointing up with the word Scroll under them sit a little under the header, centred over the 3D head, and Scroll over chevrons pointing down just over the footer, centred under it, following the head across as it moves (`framedHead` in `components/Background/headPlace.ts`); on a short window they move in only as far as the head allows (its crown and chin are fractions of the window's height in `ScrollArrows.tsx`), and under 700 pixels tall they are smaller. On a phone only the down arrow shows, small, in the header midway between the menu button and the page's title (`data-header-links` and `data-header-title`), and it hides while the phone menu is open; they sit outside the page's scroller so its fade under the header does not hide them; each glides to the post before or after, and shows only while there is a post that way (`components/PageScroller/ScrollArrows.tsx`, through `pageGlide.ts` to `SnapPager.tsx`). A post's Visit Site and View Code open in a new tab. A Web Apps post shows a small pill above its technology saying how the site was built: Hand Coded (a raised hand, in peach) for Banner Eyewear, the only one written by hand, and Vibe Coded (sparkles) for the rest, both from react-icons (`coded` in `Contents.tsx`; a post without it, as in Miscellaneous, shows none). Buttons take the head's colours, sampled from its rendered skin: its brightest highlight at rest, sweeping fast through peach, rose, purple and deep blue to its near-black navy on hover, flat with rounded corners (`.bg-gradient-button` in `styles/globals.css`). On a wide screen each post is one full page: it fills the space between the header's and the footer's fades, its images above its frosted-glass card (big rounded corners; see home.md, Theme), which spans the bottom of the post: wide and only as tall as its text, and, like the images above it, starting just right of the 3D head's face so the two line up (`components/useHeadClear.ts`; unless that leaves the post under 560 pixels); its glass runs flat past the window's right side, and on a phone past both sides, so it meets the edge square and only fades out at its top and bottom (`components/EdgeCard.tsx`), its title and description beside its technology and links, in text close to the site's normal size. The card doesn't scroll with its post: it is fixed where it rests, and only the card of the post crossing the window's middle shows, so paging sends one card on to the right, gathering speed off the window's right edge (0.8 seconds), and slides the next in from the window's left edge to its place, slowing to a stop (1.4 seconds, starting 0.7 seconds in); the card's glass stays whole all the way, and only what is on it fades: out as it leaves, and in from a fifth of the way through as it arrives. A card arrives this way when the page loads too (`animate-card-out`, `animate-card-in`, `animate-content-out` and `animate-content-in` in `tailwind.config.js`; with reduced motion the card just shows or hides and its text fades), while the images move with their posts (`components/useHeldCard.ts`). The images lie behind the card, never over its text; on a wide screen the card takes at most 55% of the post's height, longer text scrolling inside it. The images are separate tiles, not a grid: each is cropped to whichever of wide (16:9), landscape (4:3), square or portrait (3:4) is nearest its picture (from the `width` and `height` in `Contents.tsx`; a video is wide, goes first and is laid out as the largest tile, in its own shape), or one shape taller where that fills clearly more. They are packed to fill as much of their space as they can, counting more for using its height, centred, then nudged, tilted and grown a little so they overlap each other slightly, the same way on every load (`components/collageLayout.ts`). When a post comes into view (half its tiles' space clear of the top and bottom fades) each tile springs in with an overshoot from its own random side after its own random wait, then drifts gently on its own beat; when the post leaves, each pulls back and falls away; hovering a tile lifts it above the others (`components/PostCollage.tsx`, the motion in `.collage-tile` in `styles/globals.css`; with reduced motion they only fade). One notch of the wheel, one trackpad swipe (its coast included) or one press of an arrow, Page Up or Down or space glides straight to the next or previous post in 0.3 seconds, and so does a finger's swipe on a phone (40 pixels up or down; the browser's own scroll is held off meanwhile). Each page turn then plays its cards' whole change (2.1 seconds) before another can start: pushes meanwhile wait, and only the latest of them, so a burst of scrolling turns one page and a scroll back the other way turns back once the cards have settled; cards never pile up over each other. The page's first card arriving counts as a turn too (`components/PageScroller/SnapPager.tsx`, logic in `paging.ts`, `turnGate` and `TURN_MS`, tested by `paging.test.ts`); text that can still scroll inside a post scrolls first. On a short screen (800 pixels tall or less) the text is set smaller so the longest post still fits. On a phone the fades are shorter, each post still fills one screen with its image tiles above its card, its text in one column, and a post with images shows four lines of its description and hides the rest, with its technology, behind More; More gives the whole post to the text and Less brings the images back.

## Verify

Roles: none; every page is public. Content: the `Contents.tsx` beside each page and the images under `public/`.

1. On any page at 1440 by 900, click Portfolio in the header: a glass pane offers Web Apps and Miscellaneous, each opening its page with Portfolio underlined; Escape or a click outside closes it. Open `/portfolio`: it lands on Web Apps.
2. Open `/portfolio/web-apps` at 1280 by 720: a post per entry, first "This web site", each with its media and description and none cut off; one scroll moves to the next post; on the first post only Scroll's down arrow shows, clicking it moves one post, and on the last post only its up arrow shows.
3. On `/portfolio/web-apps` at 1440 by 900, one notch of the mouse wheel lands squarely on the next post with no pause, and one swipe on a trackpad moves exactly one post. Three quick notches down turn one page; a notch up while its cards change waits until the new card has settled, then turns back one page, and two cards never show over each other.
4. Open `/portfolio/miscellaneous` at 375 by 812: only the down scroll arrow shows, in the header between the menu button and the title, and tapping it moves to the next post; each post fits on one screen and one swipe moves to the next; More on Smart Bed shows its whole description and Less brings its images back.
5. Open `/portfolio/miscellaneous`: every post's image, GIF or video loads, with no broken image.

The tiles' crops and layout are covered by `components/collageLayout.test.ts`, and which header link is underlined by `components/Header/isRouteActive.test.ts`. No test that runs covers the pages: `cypress/e2e/navigation.cy.ts` visits these pages, but its selectors match nothing the components render, and no workflow runs Cypress.

## Gotchas

- A post's media `key` is a path under `public/` or, for a video, a YouTube id; a misspelled path shows a broken image with no error. An image's `width` and `height` pick its crop; left out, it is cropped square.
- `Button` is shared with [contact](contact.md).
